# Sales-Dashboard
Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard
1. Project Title / Headline
Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

A dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product demand, customer distribution, and yearly sales trends using business sales data.


2. Short Description / Purpose

The Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard is a Power BI report developed to provide a clear overview of business performance through key sales and profitability metrics. The dashboard helps users analyze total sales, profit margin, product-line performance, order quantities, top and bottom-selling products, customer distribution, and sales trends across different years and months.

It is intended for business analysts, sales managers, and decision-makers who need to monitor sales performance and identify products and product categories contributing to overall business results.


3. Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used for creating the interactive dashboard and visualizations.
📁 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used to create calculated measures such as Total Sales, Profit Margin, and other analytical metrics.
🔗 Data Modeling – Relationships were established between customers, orders, order details, products, product lines, payments, employees, and offices tables.
📄 CSV Files – Source data was provided in multiple CSV files and imported into Power BI.
📈 Power BI Visualizations – Cards, slicers, bar charts, donut chart, treemap, line chart, and map visuals were used to present the analysis interactively.

4. Data Source

The dashboard is based on a sales/business transaction dataset containing information about customers, orders, products, payments, employees, offices, and product categories.

The data is structured across multiple related tables, allowing the dashboard to analyze sales from different perspectives such as product, product line, customer, country, employee, and order year.

The major data fields include:

Customer information and country
Product and product-line information
Order dates and order years
Quantity ordered
Sales amount
Payment information
Employee and office information

This relational structure enables cross-filtering and interactive analysis throughout the dashboard.


5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

Businesses often have large amounts of sales and transaction data, making it difficult to quickly understand which products generate the most revenue, which products perform poorly, how sales change over time, and where customers are located.

The dashboard addresses this problem by bringing the major sales and profitability indicators into a single interactive report.

• Goal of the Dashboard

The main goal is to provide a centralized view of sales and profit performance and allow users to explore the data using filters such as:

City
Month
Order Year
Product Line
Employee Name

This allows users to quickly identify sales trends and compare product performance.

Key Visuals & Insights
1. Profit Margin

The dashboard displays an overall Profit Margin of 39.84%, providing a quick indication of the company's profitability relative to its sales.

2. Total Sales

The Total Sales KPI displays approximately $9.60M, giving users an immediate overview of the company's overall sales performance.

3. Most Ordered Product

The dashboard identifies 1992 Ferrari 360 Spider red as the most ordered product based on the available order data.

4. Sales Trend

The monthly sales visual provides an interactive view of sales performance over time. Users can use the Month Name filter to analyze sales for specific months.

5. Sales by Product Line

The donut chart compares sales across different product lines, including:

Classic Cars
Vintage Cars
Motorcycles
Trucks and Buses
Planes
Ships
Trains

This helps identify which product categories contribute the most to overall sales.

6. Orders by Year

The column chart shows the quantity of products ordered across 2003, 2004, and 2005, allowing users to compare order volumes between different years.

7. Top 5 Selling Products

The dashboard highlights the Top 5 products based on sales value, making it easy to identify the products contributing significantly to revenue.

8. Bottom 5 Selling Products

The Bottom 5 visual identifies products with comparatively lower sales values. This can help businesses investigate underperforming products.

9. Quantity Ordered by Product Line

The treemap visual compares the quantity ordered across product lines. Larger sections represent product lines with higher order quantities.

10. Customer Distribution by Country

The map visual provides a geographical representation of customer distribution, helping users understand the international reach of the business.


11. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes multiple slicers that allow users to dynamically analyze the data:

City – Filter results according to customer city.
Month Name – Analyze performance for specific months.
Order Year – Compare performance across different years.
Product Line – Focus on a specific product category.


Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profitability, customer distribution, product demand, and yearly performance. By identifying high-performing and low-performing products and comparing sales across product lines and time periods, businesses can use these insights to support sales planning, product analysis, inventory decisions, and performance monitoring.

Overall, the dashboard transforms raw transactional data into an interactive and easy-to-understand business intelligence report that supports data-driven decision-making.
Employee Name – Analyze sales associated with individual employees.

These filters allow users to move from a high-level business overview to more detailed analysis.
